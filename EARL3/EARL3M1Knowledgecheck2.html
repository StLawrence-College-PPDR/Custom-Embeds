Knowledge Check: Aesthetic Principles

1. Which principle suggests using a neutral background with a few well-chosen accent colours to avoid over-stimulation?

Correct Answer: Colour Generates Interest. Proper use of colour creates mood without being overpowering.

2. By eliminating clutter and highlighting children’s work, the classroom becomes a backdrop to honour all who occupy the space. This is:

Correct Answer: Displays Enhance Environment. This principle focuses on arranging storage and highlighting work to reduce clutter.

3. "Multiple sources of light create an ambiance of relaxation and contemplation." This refers to:

Correct Answer: Elements Heighten Ambiance. Supportive lighting allows children to interact creatively and calmly.

4. The environment is often called the "3rd Teacher." This concept is a hallmark of which philosophy?

Correct Answer: Reggio Emilia. This Italian town's philosophy heavily influenced the "How Does Learning Happen?" document.

5. True or False: Aesthetic principles are the "bones" or structural foundation of a space.

Correct Answer: False. The "bones" are the foundation set first; aesthetic principles are the "less tangible" elements like feel, smell, and sound.
