Knowledge Checker: Dramatic Play

1. Why should you add items like play money, writing materials, or a clock to your dramatic play center?

Correct! These items help children learn relevant topics like math and literacy while they role play.

2. Which safety items are specifically recommended for the Carpentry area?

Correct! Safety should be emphasized by providing an apron and goggles for each child.

3. What is recommended when selecting dolls for the learning center?

Correct! Multicultural dolls and inclusion equipment are great additions to the area.

4. According to the text, what often makes the "best" pretend food?

Correct! The text notes that loose parts often make the best food for imaginative play.

5. What is the primary benefit of Housekeeping Play items like mops and vacuums?

Correct! Housekeeping play helps children practice adult roles and encourages them to clean up after themselves.
