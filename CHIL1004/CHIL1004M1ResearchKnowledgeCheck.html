Knowledge Check: Research in Child Development

Answer the following questions to test your understanding of the research materials.

1. If research results are proven consistent when the study is repeated over time, the research is:
  • A) Valid
  • B) Reliable
  • C) Qualitative
2. Which approach uses theories from disciplines such as psychology, pediatrics, and sociology?
  • A) Topical
  • B) Lifespan
  • C) Multidisciplinary
3. What is a disadvantage of the "Topical" approach?
  • A) It doesn't focus on the process.
  • B) It's hard to see the interrelatedness between developmental processes.
  • C) Development stops at age 21 in this model.
4. The "Age-Stage" approach suggests development is:
  • A) Continuous and quantitative.
  • B) Discontinuous and qualitative.
  • C) Hard to see on a daily basis.
5. Studying the same group of children at ages 2, 4, and 7 is which method?
  • A) Longitudinal
  • B) Cross-Sectional
  • C) Sequential
6. In a ______ observation, the researcher manipulates the environment to elicit a specific behavior.
  • A) Naturalistic
  • B) Structured
  • C) Case Study
7. Why can't conclusions from a "Case Study" be applied to the general population?
  • A) They lack ecological validity.
  • B) They are based on self-reports only.
  • C) They only apply to the specific individual studied.
8. In an experiment, the factor being manipulated (the hypothesized cause) is the:
  • A) Dependent variable
  • B) Independent variable
  • C) Control group
9. According to ethics, do children have the right to refuse to participate if parents have said yes?
  • A) No, parents have legal authority.
  • B) Yes, children have the right to refuse.
  • C) Only in medical experiments.
10. Studies that show relationships between variables but do NOT prove cause and effect are:
  • A) Experimental Studies
  • B) Correlational Studies
  • C) Systematic Observations
