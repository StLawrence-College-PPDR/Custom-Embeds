What is Learning Connections?

Video Credits: Spark

Inclusive. Creative. SLC.

The Learning Connections Conference celebrates its tenth anniversary May 29th and 30th, 2018!

Engage. Learn. Celebrate. This year’s conference embraces our diversity through inclusivity and the creativity we inspire in each other. Staff, faculty, and administration—full-time and part-time—will come together on Kingston campus for two days of workshops, keynote speakers—artists and visionaries—panel discussions, service recognition and awards, great food and great connections.

Join us!

Your Keynote Speakers

Neil Pasricha

Author and Speaker

Neil Pasricha is one of the most popular TED speakers with his first TED Talk “The 3 A’s of Awesome” (2010) ranked as one of the ten most inspiring of all time. As a leadership keynote speaker Neil has spoken to hundreds of thousands of people around the world including Fortune 100 companies, Ivy League schools, and Royal Families in the Middle East.

Noah Scalin

Artist and Author

Noah Scalin is the first artist-in-residence at the Virginia Commonwealth University School of Business. He is creator of the Webby Award winning project Skull-A-Day and the collaborative science fiction universe & performance art project League of Space Pirates.

Travel & Accommodations

Bus

Complimentary transportation by bus to and from Cornwall campus and Brockville campus is provided throughout the day, each day of the conference. Click the link below to download the bus schedule.

Bus Schedule (PDF)

Residence

Out of town guests staying overnight will be housed in residence rooms. Guests can check in and pick up their keys after 2:00 pm at the reception desk in residence. Check out time is 11:00 am the following day. Accommodations will be separated into two areas, please specify upon registration if you would prefer your reservation on the quiet or social floor.

Please notify Hannah Muysson (hmuysson@sl.on.ca) should you require accommodations prior to May 29th.

Special Note: For those staying overnight in Kingston, please submit an expense report with original itemized receipts to Julie Johnston, (jjohnston@sl.on.ca) SCTL, Brockville Campus.

Expense form does NOT require one-over-one approval. Reimbursement will be paid on actual amount spent to a maximum of $20.00, excluding alcohol.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who Can Attend?

Staff, faculty, and administration—full-time and part-time—whether your contract is current has just finished, is current, or will start soon. EVERYONE is welcome to attend.

NOTE: Student employees at St. Lawrence College are invited to register and attend our two keynote speaker events at the start of each day of the conference as well as the meals that will be provided each day. However, in order to facilitate free and open discussions, and to allow full engagement in the concurrent workshops, attendance is restricted to full- and part-time faculty, support staff and administrative staff.

How Many Sessions Can I Attend?

Join us for just a workshop or all of the conference. We hope that you enjoy your experience.
