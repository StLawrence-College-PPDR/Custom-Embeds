How to Use

Setup your space for your designed activity. Then fill out the text fields below the environment setup to tell us your reasoning. You can practice designing your space as many times as desired. Your practice will not be stored. Your instructor will let you know if you need to save your design for the purpose of grading. If you need to save your work follow the steps below.

Save Your Work
Step 1

Hover your mouse on the right-hand panel to view the assets you currently have at this virtual centre. You can design your center by dragging any of theses objects from the storage area on the into your room. All objects must reside within the space. Move objects forward and backwards by selecting them on the screen.

Mac

Take a screenshot by holding these three keys together; Shift, Command, and 3. Your image will save to your desktop.

PC

Take a screenshot by pressing PrtScn and the Windows key together. Your image will save to your 'Pictures > Screenshots' folder.

Step 2

Save your writen reasoning by clicking the 'create PDF' button below. Only after this is fully complete can you proceed to implementation phase.
