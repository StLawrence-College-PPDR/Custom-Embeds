Implementing Your Activity

Your instructor will provide you with further information or instructions on how to implement your activity and submit details of your implementation. Below are some tips on how you could possibly create a video of implementing your designed activity.

Example Video
Introduction

Before shooting your scene, your first clip should include your name and short explanation of what you are filming.

Camera

Use your device to shoot your video. If you can, shoot in clips and add them together at the end. This way you don't have to have it perfect in one go!

Characters

If possible, have additional people within your household play characters in your scene. Don't have anyone? Use objects like puppets, stuffed animals, or action figures.

Sound & Lighting

Ensure you shoot your video in a space where you can capture good sound and light quality. Test this out before you start.

Use Props

Be creative! Use household items, toys, or other random materials to help you recreate the scene.

Capture Interactions

Show how your students react to the activity and behaviours that you expect to see.
