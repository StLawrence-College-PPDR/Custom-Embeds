Job Fair Simulation

Welcome to SLC Early Childhood Education Virtual Center. In this virtual environment you would be able to assess your knowledge on various philosophies associated with early childhood education using an interview format.
This environment can be used as a practice activity, which means that your session will not be saved or assessed. With instructions from your professor, certain sessions or activity can be used as a basis for a graded assignment. Your professor will specify which activity you will have to save and submit for grading.
