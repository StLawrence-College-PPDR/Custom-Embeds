Recall Case Example: Chris (15 years old) (Chris Case Study Continues)

Chris' mother Joanne recently called the Children's Aid Society because she is having difficulty managing Chris' high risk behaviours and she is worried that she will not be able to continue to care for him because of the impact he is having on his younger siblings. Joanne reports that Chris has been skipping school, using drugs, and getting into fights. Joanne reports that Chris has always had some issues with impulsivity and anger but that when he uses drugs he is aggressive toward her by yelling and damaging items in the home. Joanne knows that Chris drinks alcohol with his friends and smokes some marijuana but because of his behaviours she worries that Chris might be using more serious drugs. Joanne said she also overheard Chris talking about "ice" to his friends and she is worried he might be using crystal meth as a result. Joanne reports that due to Chris' damage to the home she is also worried she might be evicted from her own home and is not sure how she will manage the financial cost of moving.
