Curriculum Planning: Key Questions

Group Participation & Engagement
  • How can the whole group participate in an activity?
  • What engaging props will I be using?
  • How can I encourage child participation?
Instructions & Contingency Planning
  • Instructions: Keep them simple and clear.
  • Refocusing: Plan and practice scenarios to regain the group's attention.
  • Back-up: Always have a contingency plan.
  • Grouping: Can you split the group into smaller numbers and repeat the activity?
  • Transitions: Plan what children who are waiting will be doing so they don't get bored or distract others.
The Role of the Adult

The adult is the facilitator of the activity. Their responsibilities include:

  • Planning based on observations of children's interests and abilities.
  • Prioritizing safety and considering individual child personalities.
  • Ensuring contingency plans are ready to be implemented.
Managing Distractions

Why are they causing distractions? Are they bored or in need of attention?

  • Consider the challenge level: If it's too hard or too easy, children lose interest.
  • Strategize how to invite "distractors" to become active participants.
  • Evaluate props: Are they helping children focus, or are they a source of distraction themselves?
Safety & Environment
  • How much space is available?
  • Is the space sufficient for the movement planned, or would an outdoor or gym space be better?
Intervention & Extending Expression

When to intervene: If children are interested but repeating the same actions over and over without progress.

How to support: If a child is struggling to be heard, draw the group's attention to them:

"What was that you suggested, [Name]? I didn't quite hear. [Other Child], did you hear what [Name] said?"
Recognizing & Supporting Interest

Active participation is not the only sign of interest. Watch for:

  • The Onlooker: Observing from a distance.
  • The Evaluator: Approaching and retreating multiple times (needs gentle encouragement).
  • The Parallel Player: A child nearby who looks up and smiles. Respond by saying: "We have room for more people!"
