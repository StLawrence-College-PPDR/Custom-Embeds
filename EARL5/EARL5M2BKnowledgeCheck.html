Knowledge Check: Dissolution of Marriage

1. Which of the following is a reason for a court to grant an Annulment based on situations existing before the marriage?

Correct Answer: A prohibited blood tie (e.g., brother and sister).

2. In terms of physical ability, which of the following is a valid reason for annulment?

Correct Answer: Impotence.

3. What is meant by "marriage under duress"?

Correct Answer: Marriage where one party felt forced against their will.

4. Which term has been referred to as the "poor man's divorce"?

Correct Answer: Desertion.

5. What is the main characteristic of "Separation"?

Correct Answer: The couple is legally married but does not cohabit.

6. According to the text, "Individualism and hedonism" are classified as what type of reasons for divorce?

Correct Answer: Societal reasons.

7. Which of the following is listed as a PERSONAL reason for divorce?

Correct Answer: Emotional immaturity and emotional problems.

8. How does the text describe "patriarchal expectations" in the context of divorce?

Correct Answer: The man should be the head of household and hold economic power.

9. According to the Vanier Institute of the Family (2011), what has happened to the divorce rate in Canada since the 1990s?

Correct Answer: It has stabilized.

10. Why is annulment considered uncommon today?

Correct Answer: Because divorce is easier and simpler to obtain.
