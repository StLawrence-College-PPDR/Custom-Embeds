Knowledge Check: Culturally Diverse Families

1. According to the text, what is often the biggest challenge for families newly arrived in Canada?

Correct! The text states language may provide the biggest challenge.

2. What is identified as one of the greatest stressors for many ethnic families who are visibly different from the majority?

Correct! Discrimination based on being visibly different is a major stressor mentioned.

3. In North American culture, the individual is seen as a separate entity. In many other cultures, decisions must involve:

Correct! Many cultures emphasize interdependence and involve older family members in decisions.

4. How does North American culture generally view problem-solving compared to "fatalistic" cultures?

Correct! North Americans believe in using reason and technology to master the environment.

5. Which culture typically views time as moving quickly with a need to "keep up with it"?

Correct! North Americans perceive time in terms of clock time and speed.

6. True or False: In some cultures, interrupting a conversation is considered acceptable.

Correct! The text states that in some cultures, there is seldom silence and interrupting is acceptable.

7. How do North Americans generally expect people to express their feelings?

Correct! Controlled expression is the North American norm.

8. How is avoiding eye contact interpreted in some cultures?

Correct! For some cultures, it is a sign of respect not to make eye contact.

9. What is "interpersonal space"?

Correct! It refers to the physical amount of space between people during interaction.

10. The conclusion suggests that while differences exist, they are very small compared to:

Correct! The text emphasizes that our similarities are greater than our differences.
