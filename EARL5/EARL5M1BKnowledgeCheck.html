Knowledge Check: Models for Understanding Families

1. What is the primary purpose of a "conceptual framework" in the context of family systems?
Correct Answer: To organize factors and thoughts around how a family functions
2. Which family form includes stepparents, step-siblings, or half-siblings?
Correct Answer: Blended family
3. Socioeconomic Status (SES) is primarily determined by which three factors?
Correct Answer: Income, educational level, and occupations
4. A parent loses their job but chooses to see it as an opportunity to return to school. This is an example of:
Correct Answer: Reframing
5. Which family subsystem focuses specifically on parent and child interactions?
Correct Answer: Parental subsystem
6. A child who is overprotected and has no emotional or physical privacy likely comes from a family experiencing:
Correct Answer: Enmeshment
7. In a family where roles are strictly defined and rules are unchangeable, the adaptability style is:
Correct Answer: Rigid
8. In Bronfenbrenner's Ecological Approach, the interactions *between* microsystems (like home and school) is called the:
Correct Answer: Mesosystem
9. A government decision that affects a child's school program (even if the child never meets the government officials) is part of the:
Correct Answer: Exosystem
10. What is the recommended strategy for communicating with a family that has a "Chaotic" operating style?
Correct Answer: Communicate clearly with each member (child and adult) verbally and in writing
