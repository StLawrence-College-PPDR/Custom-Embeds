Cell Division Review

1. During mitosis, the chromosome number stays the same, while meiosis reduces it by half. Why is this reduction important for sexual reproduction, and what would happen if it didn’t occur?
Key Concept: Meiosis reduces chromosome number by half so fertilization restores the correct diploid number. Without this reduction, chromosome numbers would double every generation.
2. Meiosis creates genetically unique gametes, while mitosis produces identical daughter cells. What specific events during meiosis lead to genetic variation, and why is this variation beneficial for a species?
Key Concept: Crossing over and independent assortment during meiosis create genetic variation, which increases a species’ ability to adapt and survive.
3. A cut on your skin heals through mitosis, while the formation of sperm or egg cells requires meiosis. How does each process support these different biological needs?
Key Concept: Mitosis produces identical cells for growth and repair, while meiosis produces gametes for sexual reproduction.
